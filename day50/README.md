# 🚀 Day 50/60 – ABTalks 60 Day Claude Challenge

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 50** of my **ABTalks 60 Day Claude Challenge**!

Today's project focuses on designing and developing **Defend Your Experience**—an AI-powered interview preparation application that helps users confidently defend every claim they make about their experience.

Unlike traditional interview practice tools, this application acts as an intelligent skeptic, continuously asking personalized follow-up questions based on the user's resume, portfolio, LinkedIn profile, projects, or other uploaded documents.

The objective is simple:

> **Don't just write your experience. Learn how to defend it.**

---

## ✨ Features

* 📄 Upload Resume, Portfolio, LinkedIn Profile, Research Paper, or Bio
* 🤖 AI-generated adaptive interview questions
* 🎯 Personalized follow-up questions based on previous answers
* 🔍 Detects vague statements and weak claims
* 📊 Defense confidence analysis
* 📈 Progress tracking dashboard
* 🛡️ Experience Defense Report
* 💾 Local storage support
* 📤 Export interview reports
* 📱 Fully responsive design
* 🎨 Premium modern UI/UX
* ⚡ Graceful API error handling

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Upload your experience document.
2. AI extracts every meaningful claim.
3. Each claim becomes an interview topic.
4. The AI interviewer challenges your answers.
5. Follow-up questions become more detailed over time.
6. Weak areas are identified.
7. Receive a complete **Defense Report** with personalized improvement suggestions.
